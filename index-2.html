Born in Lahore, carried up the mountains

The Last Tribe began as a single room in Lahore built around one idea: that Pakhtun and Afghan cooking deserved a table as considered as its flavour. Slow-cooked Shinwari karahi, Kabuli pulao layered with raisins and toasted almonds, and naan pulled straight from a clay tandoor.

The Giga Skywalk address in Islamabad's DHA Phase II is TLT's rooftop chapter — the same fire and rug-lined aesthetic, raised five floors into the open air, with the city instead of the mountains as the backdrop.
